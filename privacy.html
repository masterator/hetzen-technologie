2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details: your name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Project details: what you need, your budget, timeline, content, files and feedback.
  • Billing details: what appears on quotes and invoices, and records of payments. We do not store card or bank login details.
  • Messages: what you send us on WhatsApp, email, phone or through our website form.
  • Access details for your project, such as hosting or domain accounts, only when you choose to give them so we can do the work. We ask you to share access through a secure method, never in a normal message.

Information from other places

  • Public business information, such as your website or Google Business Profile, when we prepare a quote.
  • Information from the people who referred you to us, with your knowledge.

Information collected automatically

Our website does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure.

4. Do you have to give us your information?

It's your choice. But we need at least a name and a way to contact you to reply, and enough project detail to quote accurately. If you don't share something we need, we may not be able to quote or finish the work. Tax law requires us to keep invoice records.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell or rent your information. We only share it when needed to do the work, with:

  • Service providers we use to run our business:
    • Supabase, which stores our business records.
    • GitHub, which hosts our website and code.
    • Meta (WhatsApp), Google (email) and Cloudflare, which carry our messages and network traffic.
    They may only use it to provide their service to us.
  • Services you ask us to set up for you, such as a domain registrar, hosting company or payment provider.
  • People required by law, such as SARS or a court, if we are legally required to.
  • Our accountant or legal adviser, who must keep it confidential.

6. Information stored outside South Africa

Some of our providers store data in other countries. For example, our business records are stored by Supabase in Ireland (EU), and GitHub, Google and Meta run servers worldwide. Section 72 of POPIA allows this where the receiving country or the provider's binding terms give protection substantially similar to POPIA. The EU's GDPR is one such law. By using our services you understand that this happens.

7. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't go ahead: up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Customer, project, quote and invoice records: 5 years after the last invoice, because the Tax Administration Act requires this.
  • Passwords and access you gave us for a project: removed or handed back at the end of the project, and you should change them.
  • Anything you ask us to delete: deleted, unless the law says we must keep it.

8. How we protect it

  • Our business portal needs a password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (two-step verification).
  • The database refuses to show records to anyone who isn't signed in as the owner, even if they have the website's public key.
  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Our admin accounts on Supabase and GitHub have two-step verification switched on.
  • Logging out of the portal clears customer data from the device.
  • We regularly check our systems against the security checklist on our Security page.

No online system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, up-to-date steps as section 19 of POPIA requires.

9. When you give us your customers' data

If we build a system that holds personal information about your customers or staff, you are the responsible party for that data. We act as your "operator" under sections 20 and 21 of POPIA. That means we:

  • only use it to do the work you asked for;
  • keep it confidential and secure;
  • tell you straight away if we think it has been accessed without permission;
  • delete or return it when the project ends.

For bigger projects we sign a separate written operator agreement with you.

10. Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge:

  • whether we hold information about you, and for a copy of it;
  • to correct anything that's wrong or out of date;
  • to delete information we no longer need, or that we shouldn't have;
  • to stop using your information, including for marketing (you can object);
  • to withdraw consent you gave earlier.

Send your request by WhatsApp or email. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We reply within 30 days. For formal access requests, see our PAIA Manual.

12. Cookies and tracking

Our public website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Our business portal stores a sign-in session and a working copy of records in your browser so it can work offline. That storage is only for signed-in users and is cleared when you log out.

13. Marketing messages

We only send marketing by WhatsApp, SMS or email in two cases: if you've agreed to it, or if you're an existing customer and the message is about services like the ones you used. Every message tells you how to stop them. Reply "STOP" and we'll remove you.

14. If something goes wrong

If we believe someone has got into personal information without permission, we will tell the Information Regulator and the people affected as soon as reasonably possible. We'll explain what happened, what it means for you and what you can do, as section 22 of POPIA requires.

15. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top whenever we change this policy. If a change affects you significantly, we'll let our customers know directly.
